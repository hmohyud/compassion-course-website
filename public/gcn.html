Common questions

Quick fixes for the things people run into most.

It says “Incorrect email or password” or it can’t find my account

This usually means one of two things:

1. You haven’t created an account yet — go back up and use the green Sign Up button.

2. You signed up with a different email address — try the other email addresses you use, especially the one where your course emails arrive.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in page, click “Forgot your password?” (just below the Sign In button). Enter your email and you’ll receive a reset link. Check your spam folder if it doesn’t arrive within a few minutes.

When I try to sign up, it says my email is already taken

Good news — that means you already have an account. Use the blue Sign In button instead. If you don’t remember your password, use “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page.

Which email address should I use?

Always use the email address where you receive your Compassion Course emails. Using a different address can create a second, separate account and cause confusion later.

I signed in, but I don’t see the course community

Make sure you joined through our invitation link — the green Sign Up button above. If you created an account some other way, sign in first and then click that same Sign Up link; it will connect your account to our community.
